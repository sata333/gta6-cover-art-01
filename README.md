**GTA VI Cover Art Studio** is a client-side, zero-dependency static web application that transforms any photograph into authentic, high-impact Grand Theft Auto VI multi-panel poster cover art. 

Featuring iconic comic-inked panels with heavy gutters, true-to-life Vice City sunset and synthwave neon grading, pop-art posterization, official-style Roman numeral "VI" logo headers, and mature rating badges — fully rendered offline with the HTML5 Canvas API.

---

## Key Features

- **Iconic GTA Multi-Panel Collages**: Choose from Solo, Duo, Trio, Quad, and the legendary 6-panel Classic poster layout.
- **Vice City Signature Colour Grading**:
  - *Vice Sunset*: Tangerine orange, hot magenta, and palm-tree sunshine glow.
  - *Neon Night*: Electric ocean cyan, laser pink, and midnight purple.
  - *Golden Hour*: Crisp daytime contrast with radiant warmth.
  - *Vice Noir*: High-contrast comic monochrome with hot pink accents.
- **Rockstar Comic & Pop-Art Inking**: Simulates hand-drawn comic illustrations with deep contrast and halftone texture passes.
- **Official Branding & Rating Badges**: Authentic GTA VI Roman numeral "VI" logo header and ESRB/PEGI-style Mature 17+ corner box.
- **Interactive Multi-Crop Studio**: Drag to reposition crops, scroll-wheel zoom per panel, and batch-fill across all panels.
- **High-Resolution Multi-Format Export**: One-click PNG export in Square (1080×1080), Portrait (1080×1350), or Landscape (1920×1080).
- **100% Offline & Pure Web**: Zero build step, zero external CDN dependencies, instant response.

## Quick Start

```sh
node serve.ts
```
